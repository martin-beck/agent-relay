# Contributing

All contributions, including AI-assisted and autonomous work, must follow the
[canonical development process](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md).

Read every project document that process routes to for the current change. Do
not maintain a second copy of its workflow, restrictions, or verification rules
in this file.

Contributions may be proposed through a focused GitHub issue or pull request.
Keep each change narrowly scoped, add or update its tests and documentation, and
run the applicable gates from [Building](docs/BUILDING.md) and
[Quality and safety](docs/QUALITY.md).

Commits must be attributable, signed, and include a Developer Certificate of
Origin sign-off. By adding a sign-off, a contributor certifies that they have
the right to submit the work under this repository's MIT License. Do not submit
credentials, private host or account details, prompts, transcripts, proprietary
material, generated build output, or third-party code without compatible terms
and preserved attribution.

Participation is governed by the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
